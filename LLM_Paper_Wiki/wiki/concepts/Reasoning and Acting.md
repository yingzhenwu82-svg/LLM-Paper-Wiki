# Reasoning and Acting
Reasoning-and-acting designs interleave internal planning or reasoning with environment actions and observations. ReAct is a representative framework.

## Simple loop
1. Interpret current task and observation.
2. Decide next step.
3. Execute an action.
4. Read observation.
5. Continue or stop.

This is a conceptual overview; implementations differ in whether and how reasoning traces are exposed.
